# Instantly

> "Reach Clients On Autopilot — Use Instantly AI to find perfect leads, create AI Sales Agents and automate your outreach & sales."
> — https://instantly.ai/

**One-line read:** A cold-email *sending machine*. Its real product is
deliverability — unlimited mailboxes, warmup, and inbox rotation so that high
volume still lands. Everything else is attached to that.

## What they sell

Volume email with the deliverability plumbing that volume requires: unlimited
email accounts, an automated warmup network, inbox rotation, inbox-placement
testing, a lead database, an email verifier, a unified inbox, and a light CRM. The
2026 layer on top is agents — an **AI Sales Agent**, an **AI Reply Agent**, and a
**Lead Finder Agent**. They claim "50,000+ sales teams," "45,000+ outbound teams,"
and **"50B+ emails"** sent.

## Use cases they lead with

The sitemap is the finding: **1,930 URLs, of which 1,395 are blog** and only *one*
is a product page. There is **no `use-cases/` bucket and no real solutions
section**. Instantly's go-to-market is programmatic SEO — hundreds of
`/prompt/personalized-cold-email-opener-for-<vertical>` pages — not a use-case map.

| their label | what it means | source |
|---|---|---|
| Outreach | run the sequence | https://instantly.ai/ |
| Deliverability / Email Warmup | keep the domain out of spam | same |
| Inbox Placement | test where mail lands | same |
| Lead Database + Email Verification | source and clean the list | same |
| AI Sales Agent / AI Reply Agent | draft, and respond to replies | same |
| CRM | track what came back | same |

**What the site does not claim:** anything past the reply. No website work, no
conversion, no experimentation. Instantly's world ends when someone answers.

## Pricing and the metering unit

Two ladders. **Outreach** plans meter sending; **Bundle** plans add the agents.

| plan | price/mo | metered on | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Growth | $47 | 5,000 emails, 1,000 contacts, unlimited mailboxes | https://instantly.ai/pricing |
| Hypergrowth | $97 | 125,000 emails, 25,000 contacts | same |
| Lightspeed | $358 | 500,000 emails, 100,000 contacts | same |
| Starter (bundle) | $94 ($85 annual) | 5,000 emails, 1,500 credits, AI Sales Agent | same |
| Scale (bundle) | $194 ($175 annual) | 100,000 emails, 5,000 credits, AI Reply Agent | same |
| Agency (bundle) | $555 ($500 annual) | 500,000 emails, 10,000 credits | same |
| Enterprise | custom | 500,000+ emails, private deliverability network | same |

**The metering unit is `emails sent`.** Not seats — *sends*. Instantly thinks it is
selling **outbound volume**, and prices accordingly.

## Who buys it

Agencies, freelancers, founders, and lean sales teams — the plan literally named
"Agency" is the giveaway. Logos claimed on the homepage: HP, Sony, Stripe,
Flexport, Ramp, Linear, Lovable, Revolut.

## What search found that the site didn't

- **Deliverability is both the product and the top complaint.** Multiple 2026
  r/coldemail threads report warmup "heat scores" reading 90+ while live campaigns
  hit **30–40% spam placement**; done-for-you domains arriving with poor reputation
  is the single most-cited Q1 2026 complaint pattern.
  https://www.salesforge.ai/blog/instantly-ai-review
- One first-page result is literally titled *"Instantly.ai Just Admitted Their
  Warmup Network Got My Brand New Domains Reputation Blocked."*
- **Support delays during deliverability crises** recur in 1-star Trustpilot reviews.
  https://trustpilot.com/reviews/68de47481356955134584932
- Instantly has itself been called out publicly for spamming — a LinkedIn post
  documents receiving mail "from 8 different domains."
  https://www.linkedin.com/posts/paulsullivan6_instantlyai-have-emailed-me-from-8-different-activity-7394701123485589507-hI3Z
- **They integrate with Clay and Apollo** rather than replacing them
  (`/marketplace/clay`, `/marketplace/apollo`) — confirming the stack model: Clay
  enriches, Instantly sends.

**Who the market compares them to:** Smartlead, lemlist, Saleshandy, Salesforge,
Maildoso, Mailreach.

## Where we overlap

Effectively none. Envoy explicitly **never sends** — the compliance boundary is a
human on the send side. Instantly's entire product *is* the send side. We are on
opposite sides of the one line we drew on purpose.

## How EarlBear is different

| axis | Instantly | EarlBear |
|---|---|---|
| the job it does | deliver high-volume cold email to the inbox | run a storefront's growth loop |
| tool vs. outcome | tool — sending infrastructure you operate | outcome — the fleet does the work |
| where the human sits | configures the machine, then it sends autonomously | reviews and *sends*; the agent never does |
| unit of work sold | emails sent | store run / experiment shipped |
| what happens after the click | nothing — the reply is the finish line | that is where our work starts |
| who the buyer is | an agency or a founder buying volume | a store owner buying growth |

Instantly's core competence — getting a lot of mail into a lot of inboxes — is a
thing we deliberately do not do. Our one outreach agent drafts and queues, and a
person sends. If we ever look like Instantly, we've made a mistake.
